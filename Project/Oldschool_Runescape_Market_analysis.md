# Oldschool Runescape Grand Exchange Market Analysis Project

## Project goal
This project focuses on utilizing Python and MYSQL to analyze market trends in the grand exchange based off real life events occuring. Currently this project has two main subprojects. The first subproject is analyzing buy and sell volumes and prices of specific weapons that are called godswords. They are highly sought after weapons that are traded as the complete weapon and their parts. The goal of this subprojct was that by analyzing market trends using statistical analysis and regression we could predict optimum times to purchases and sell these weapons based off real world events. Currently, the godsword project is put on the back burner while I focus on the other subproject.

The second subproject is currently the main focus of this oldschool runescape grand exchange market analysis project. In this project we analyzed trends in common and necessary items that are traded in high volume on the grand exchange. Specifically we focused on looking at the effect that the US goverments bombing of the Venezuela's powergrid had on the active bot population by analzying player count and trade prices and volumes. We found that due to the powergrid disruption in Venezuela following the jan 3rd 2026 bombing, player count and several items buy volumes were statistically different.
